# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step, hosted on Vercel. Payments through Stripe Payment Links (owner chose this option on Nov 13 2026). The catalog lives in one data file, `data/works.js`.

## Users

People who want to buy a painting or a print from the artist: visitors arriving from her social media or word of mouth, often on a phone. They want to see the work large, learn its size and price, and buy without creating an account.

Inferred, not yet confirmed: the buyer audience includes dog owners, because her newest piece is a dog portrait.

## Product Purpose

A website where the artist shows her paintings and sells them. Success is a visitor finding a piece, understanding exactly what they get (original or print, size, price) and paying for it.

## Positioning

The artist paints wilderness scenes and dog portraits (owner: "she recently did a piece of outside wilderness but her newest was a dog portrait"). The site sells her own work directly, with no marketplace in between.

## Operating Context

Each piece gets its own Stripe Payment Link. Originals are one of a kind and show as sold once bought. Prints come in set sizes and can sell many copies. The site owner (the agency) maintains the catalog file on the artist's behalf.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sells original pieces and prints. No cart: one piece per checkout.
- Commissions are not offered on the site (not selected by the owner).
- Undecided: artist's public name (working name "Mira K", taken from the repository name), contact email, Instagram, shipping regions, return policy, print sizes and all prices.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. No artwork photos, bio, titles, sizes or prices have been supplied. Everything on the site today is sample content marked as such, and must be replaced before launch. Do not present sample pieces, prices or policies as real.

## Product Principles

1. The artwork is the loudest thing on every page.
2. A buyer always knows what they are getting: original or print, size, price, availability.
3. Buying takes one decision and one button.
4. The artist's helper can add or sell a piece by editing one file.
