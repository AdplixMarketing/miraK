Shipping and returns

Originals

Original paintings ship within 5 business days of your order. Each one is wrapped, boxed and insured for its full price, and you will get a tracking number by email.

Prints

Prints are made to order and ship within 7 to 10 business days, flat or rolled depending on the size.

Where we ship

Orders ship within the United States. For anywhere else, send a message first and we will work out the cost together.

Returns

If an original is not right once you see it in person, tell us within 14 days of delivery and send it back in its original packing for a refund of the purchase price. Return shipping is paid by the buyer.

Prints are made for you, so they cannot be returned, but a print that arrives damaged will be replaced at no cost. Send a photo of the damage within 14 days.

Payment

Payments are handled by Stripe. Card details never touch this website.
