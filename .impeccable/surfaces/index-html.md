# Site brief: Mira K shop (all pages)

Scope: whole site (home, paintings, painting detail, about, contact, policies). Visitor mode: Experience. The paintings lead; buying is one button.

Audience and job: people arriving from social media, mostly on phones, who want to see a piece large, learn size and price, and buy an original or a print.

Unresolved: real artwork, artist name, email, policies, prices. All current content is sample.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Painter's Field Kit. Every painting is shown with the paint it was mixed from. Refuses the white portfolio grid where color belongs only to the images.

OWN-WORLD: A gessoed panel ground (cool white), ivory black ink, and no fixed accent: the five pigments of the painting in view supply all color, laid down as rough brushed swatches with penciled pigment names. Buttons take the deepest pigment. Sharp corners on everything except the brushed swatches. Bricolage Grotesque for type, a pen script only for pigment notes.

STORY: The visitor sees a painting, sees its colors pulled out beside it, understands whether the original or prints are available and at what price, and buys.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One plain sentence across the top, "Paintings of wild places and good dogs." Below it the newest painting (up to 56 percent width; a tall piece is narrower so the whole painting fits above the fold), a full height column of its five swatches beside it, then title, medium, size, price and the View painting button.

Signature interaction, "Loading the palette": whenever a painting comes into view its five swatches are brushed in left to right, one after another. Motion grammar: brush wipes only, nothing else moves.

ADDED: a to scale drawing of the original above a sofa on each painting page, because buyers need to judge size.

FORM: Painter's field kit and swatch card, chosen by the owner from the presented directions (not the rolled assignment). Seed key b9df705c.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
