About the artist

Mira K paints wild places and the dogs who love them. Her landscapes start as quick sketches made outdoors, on ridges and lake shores, and are finished in the studio.

The dog portraits began with her own dog and kept going. Each one is painted from time spent with the animal, backed up by photographs for the moments when the sitter wanders off.

Every painting on this site is sold directly by the artist. Have a question about a piece? Send a message.
